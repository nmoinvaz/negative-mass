# Three mixed-sign scalars on the wall for an arbitrary profile: the ideal step

November 2026, issue [#157](https://github.com/nmoinvaz/negative-mass/issues/157). Script
[`three_scalars_ideal.py`](three_scalars_ideal.py), its stdout [`three_scalars_ideal_output.txt`](three_scalars_ideal_output.txt).
It builds on [`three_scalars.md`](three_scalars.md) (#77, whose C5 it addresses) and on [`two_scalars.md`](two_scalars.md) (#65).

Status words: **proved** (by hand), **checked symbolically** (sympy, exact, with a control that fails), **certified**
(computer-assisted, with a certificate that sympy verifies without Singular; trusted code named), **computed** (Singular's
output over ℚ or modulo a prime, without a certificate), **conditional** (hypothesis named), **numerical evidence**.

## Answer

**Not decided exactly. Conditionally, no.** The computation that would certify the unit ideal did not finish, and the ideal
is not the unit ideal. What finished:

- At λ = 0 an open off-axis box of the wall closes iff h is flat on it (#77, Lemma C). Then at every r of the box the jets
  u_k = r^k w^(k)(r), with u1 = r w′ ≠ 0, satisfy every coefficient of c^k in N′₂₂ = det(h)² Ric(h)_θθ / (1 − c²)³ (**proved**,
  below). Call I₂₂ the ideal in ℚ[s, u0..u4] generated by those five coefficients and 1 − s u1.
- I₂₂ is **not** the unit ideal. Its variety contains L = {2u0 = u1, u1 + u2 = 0} (u1, u3, u4 free), the jets for which
  det h vanishes for every θ, #65's locus (**checked symbolically**, with an exact point).
- No profile with u1 ≠ 0 lies on L (**certified**: the ODE step, a Singular unit-ideal certificate checked in sympy).
- Every generator of I₂₂ lies in (a, b²), a = 2u0 − u1, b = u1 + u2, with exact decompositions, and the lowest-order parts
  have rank 2 at every u1 ≠ 0, so I₂₂ = (a, b²) in the formal neighborhood of L (**checked symbolically**).
- Singular's `std` of I₂₂ modulo p equals (a, b², 1 − s u1) for p = 32003, 65521, 1000003 and 2147483629 (**computed**
  modulo primes: **numerical evidence**). The exact `lift` (cofactors) over ℚ, and `std` over ℚ, each stopped at the 1 h limit.

So: **conditional on I₂₂ = (a, b², 1 − s u1) over ℚ** (hypothesis H), no C⁴ profile closes on an open off-axis box of the
constant-speed wall at λ = 0. Without H the outcome is the third one the issue allows: the elimination does not finish. The
unit ideal after saturating by r w′ alone is not what the computation gives, since V(I₂₂) = L ≠ ∅. The λ ≠ 0 branches are
not touched.

## The reduction (proved)

Let w ∈ C⁴, h the orbit-space metric of #77 on (t, r, θ), and N_ab = det(h)² Ric(h)_ab. h has entries in r, c = cos θ,
S = sin θ and W_k = w^(k)(r), k ≤ 2, and N_ab is a polynomial in them, linear in W4.

1. **Weights.** N_ab has weights r = 1, W_k = −k, so r = 1 loses nothing: the unknowns are u_k = r^k W_k, and the saturation by
   r w′ is the saturation by u1. (`num_weights`: N(r₀; W) = r₀^m N(1; r₀^k W_k), m = −10, −10, −9, −10, −9, −8 for 00, 01, 02,
   11, 12, 22, at 3 points.)
2. **Axis.** Every N_ab is divisible by (1 − c²)², N₂₂ by (1 − c²)³ (exact division, asserted). On a box off the axis
   1 − c² = S² ≠ 0, so N′ = N/(1 − c²)^k vanishes with N.
3. **Trigonometric polynomial.** At fixed r, N′_ab = A(c) + S B(c) mod S² = 1 − c² is a trigonometric polynomial in θ. If it
   vanishes on an interval it vanishes identically, so every coefficient of c^k and S c^k vanishes. Those 34 coefficients of
   the six components generate J_all, and at any single θ₀ the numerators are ℚ-combinations of them (`coef_reassemble`).
4. **Flatness.** If h is a metric and Ric(h) = 0 on the box, N = 0 there, so J_all(u(r)) = 0 at every r of the box, with
   u1(r) ≠ 0 where f′ ≠ 0. A unit certificate Σ c_i f_i + c₀ (1 − s u1) = κ ≠ 0 for any subset of J_all would then give
   0 = κ at s = 1/u1. For this reason no saturation by det h is needed, and none is used.

I₂₂ is the subset of J_all that comes from N₂₂ (no W4, quadratic in W3).

## The numerators (checked symbolically at exact points)

`ric_num` computes N from the values and the first and second derivatives of h with the adjugate:
Γ^a_bc = GN/det, ∂_e Γ^a_bc = (T1 + det T2)/det², so det² Ric is a polynomial (**proved**: an algebraic identity). h is
`orbit_metric` of `wall_frame` from `three_scalars.py`; derivatives are its `D_jets`. At r = 1, N has 2562, 2238, 2202,
1834, 1648, 1585 terms for 00, 01, 02, 11, 12, 22, and 1669, 1456, 1368, 1140, 964, 746 after the axis division; det h has
42 terms.

- `num_cross`: equal to `three_scalars.ricci_pt` at 6 exact points, 6 components each. `ricci_pt` shares h with this script.
- `num_indep`: equal at 3 exact points to a route that shares nothing with `wall_frame` or `ric_num`: the coordinate Ricci
  tensor of the 4D metric −dt² + (w dt + cos θ dr − r sin θ dθ)² + (sin θ dr + r cos θ dθ)² + r² sin²θ dϕ² with a generic
  function w(r), its own Christoffel code, and its own 3D Ricci routine on numbers. It does share sympy and the definition
  of the wall metric (the same as `ind77.py`'s).

The numerators are exact at those 9 points; the claim that they are the numerators for every jet rests on the algebraic
identity and on `ric_num` being the code that evaluates it.

## Planted truth, before the wall

- `planted_blocks`: #77's P1 (cone) and P2 (cosh) quotient blocks, taken as `closure3` takes them from
  `planted_geometry`, go through `ric_num`: all six numerators are 0. D1's block (fiber R²(1 + R²/10)) has numerators
  (00), (11), (12), (22) nonzero.
- `planted_family`: h = diag(−1, 1, G²), G = r (1 + w). N_rr = −G³G″, N_θθ = −G⁵G″ with G″ = 2W1 + W2, exactly. The numerators
  also vanish on the spurious set u0 = −1 where det h = 0, so the saturation by det h is needed there and, divided out,
  leaves ±(2W1 + W2). Singular on it: not unit, dim 4.
- `planted_root`: R1's generators with constants shifted so that a random rational jet u* has u1* ≠ 0 as a root. Not unit,
  certified by the point. (Singular timed out at 10 min on it, as it does on R1 itself over ℚ.)

## The structure of I₂₂ (checked symbolically)

Coordinates a = 2u0 − u1, b = u1 + u2. Each N′₂₂ coefficient g_j (j = 0..4, in c⁰, c², c⁴, c⁶, c⁸) has no monomial
a⁰b⁰ or a⁰b¹, so g_j = α_j a + β_j b² exactly. Their lowest-order parts, α_j and β_j at a = b = 0, are polynomials in u1 only
(free of u3 and u4):

| j | A_j(u1) | B_j(u1) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | −8 u1⁹ | −4 u1⁶ |
| 1 | −u1⁹ (u1² − 4)(u1² + 4) | −2 u1⁶ (u1⁴ + u1² − 4) |
| 2 | −u1⁹ (u1² − 4)² / 2 | −u1⁸ (u1⁴ + 4u1² − 16) / 4 |
| 3 | 0 | −u1¹⁰ (u1² − 4) / 8 |
| 4 | 0 | 0 |

The gcd of the ten 2 × 2 minors A_j B_k − A_k B_j is u1¹⁵, so the 5 × 2 matrix has rank 2 at every u1 ≠ 0. Hence near L,
(a, b²) ⊂ I₂₂ ⊂ (a, b²): I₂₂ is (a, b²) in the formal neighborhood of L. For every pair, Δ_jk a and Δ_jk b² lie in I₂₂ with
cofactors (β_k, −β_j) and (α_j, −α_k), Δ_jk = α_j β_k − α_k β_j. All five generators vanish identically on L, det h vanishes
identically on L (`det_on_L`), and the exact point u = (1/2, 1, −1, 0, 0) lies in V(I₂₂) with s = 1.

**What is not established.** That no point with u1 ≠ 0 lies outside L, that is, a ∈ I₂₂ and b² ∈ I₂₂ over ℚ. A point off L
would have rank M ≤ 1 for the 5 × 2 matrix M(u) of the α_j, β_j, and a b² ≠ 0 vector in its kernel. The local rank-2
statement does not exclude it; only the global Gröbner basis does, and that is H.

## The ODE step (certified)

On L, F1 = 2u0 − u1 = 2w − r w′ = 0 and F2 = u1 + u2 = r (r w′)′ = 0. With θ = r d/dr, θ u_k = k u_k + u_{k+1}
(`ode_step`: from u_k = r^k w^(k), by sympy), so θ F1 = u1 − u2 and F2 + θ F1 = 2 u1. Both relations on an interval give
u1 = 0, against u1 ≠ 0 (**proved**; #65's argument in these variables). The same statement is an ideal: Singular's
`liftstd` gives cofactors (0, s, s, 2) for (F1, F2, θF1, 1 − s u1) with Σ c_i f_i = 2, which `cert_verify_ODE` checks exactly
in sympy (**certified**; Singular and sympy trusted).

## Lemmas stated for Lean (#135)

1. **(T)** A real polynomial P(c) + S Q(c) on the circle c² + S² = 1 that vanishes on an arc has P = Q = 0. Proved.
2. **(W)** N_ab(λr, λ^(−k) W_k) = λ^m N_ab(r, W_k) with m as in `num_weights`, and N_ab = (1 − c²)^k N′_ab with N′ a polynomial.
   Checked symbolically at exact points; proved by weights of the metric.
3. **(R)** If h is a C² metric on a box with Ric(h) = 0, then N = 0 there, and J_all(u(r)) = 0 at every r. Proved.
4. **(K)** On a ring over ℚ, Σ c_i f_i = κ ≠ 0 with f_i, c_i given implies that no point with u1 ≠ 0 satisfies f_i = 0. Proved;
   the ODE instance is checked.
5. **(L)** w ∈ C² with 2w = r w′ and r w′ + r² w″ = 0 on an interval implies w′ = 0. Proved.
6. **(H)** I₂₂ : u1^∞ = (2u0 − u1, (u1 + u2)²). Computed mod four primes; open over ℚ.
