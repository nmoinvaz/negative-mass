November 2026, issue [#183](https://github.com/nmoinvaz/negative-mass/issues/183). Script
[`conical_offgrid.py`](conical_offgrid.py) (a new script, not a mode of `conical_global.py`), its stdout

**conjecture**. Nothing here is certified: no enclosure is computed. Every number quoted below is in the output file.

## Answer

**No regular crossing was found.** On 818 points off #167's grid (|F_∞| from 0.001 to 128 outside [0.25, 4], or |C| from
10⁴ to 10⁸), on the 818 mirror points, and along five lines of 607 points between them, the inward walk from the series of #159
never reached η = 0 with F′ bounded. Every point ends at a first singular η* of one of two kinds, or at the floor η = 10⁻⁶ with F′
diverging; none is `THROUGH`, so there is no family of crossings to describe and none to call even. All of it is **numerical
evidence**, on a finite set of points.

## 1. What is run

The family of #159, F = F_∞ − 2F_∞/η² + (C + F_∞(F_∞² + 7) ln η)/η⁴ + …, is the series of #167 through η⁻¹⁷ (36 terms with
logarithms, `src/tables.rs`), with every coefficient now an exact sum of 8 f64 parts (`src/tables_hp.rs`, generated by
`conical_offgrid.py --gen-hp`, equal to the regenerated table) so that it can be evaluated in double-, quad- and octo-double lanes.

**Grid** (fixed before the first run of record, raw README): C ∈ {0, ±1, ±3, ±10, …, ±3000, ±1e4, …, ±1e8} (35 values); region A: #167's
eleven F_∞ with |C| ≥ 1e4 (198 points); region B: |F_∞| ∈ {0.2, 0.15, 0.1, 0.07, 0.05, 0.03, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005, 0.002, 0.001} with
|C| ≤ 1e8|F_∞| (305); region C: F_∞ ∈ {5, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 64, 128}, all of C (315). The rule |C| ≤ 1e8|F_∞| was set from the series
alone (`dev_00_startprobe.log`). The mirror (−F_∞, −C) is run for every point.

**Start rule.** The series is evaluated at L = 200·4ᵏ, k = 0 … 6 (200 … 819200), and the start is the smallest L with
|N|/Σ|terms of N| ≤ 1e-20 at (F, F′, F″) and L. Every point has one: the chosen L is 200 at 52 points, 800 at 143, 3200 at 189, 12800 at 180,
51200 at 140, 204800 at 108 and 819200 at 6, and the worst start residual is 9.0e-21. #167's literal start η = 200 meets the bar at 52 of the 818 points and fails at the other 766.

**Walker.** #184's `hp_walk`: Taylor steps in the variable s = (x − x₀)/l with l a power of two (no underflow at η = 10⁶), order 30,
tolerance 1e-32, in double-double with N constant along the flow (`conserve`); quad-double (order 60, 1e-64) is the cross-check. Each
step solves order k of N = 0 for F″ₖ with one division by ∂N/∂F″ (#167 §1). The locator is `walk.rs`' Domb–Sykes fit of the ratios of the Taylor
coefficients of F, F′, F″ over the last 10 orders (the last 33 in quad-double), five consecutive real estimates ahead with R ≤ 1e-12·max(1, |x|)
(the pass of record; the first pass is R ≤ 1e-8·max(1, |x|)); the step guard is 1e-15|x|. A singular η* is x − R of the last estimate.

**Error bars, each named.**

| quantity | bar | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| η* | ≤ 1.2e-12 over the 818 points (the criterion is 1e-8·max(1, η*)) | the **spread** (max − min) of the last five estimates, over steps; not an enclosure |
| start | η* identical to 0.0 between L and 4L at 812 points (6 have no second start) | the **spread over the start radius**: series truncation and rounding together |
| precision | η* ≤ 4.1e-13 relative, p at the floor to 1e-12, between double- and quad-double at 142 points | the **difference between two lanes** (the locator's own extrapolation noise, not integration) |
| walker | η* identical to 0.0 between `project` and `conserve` over the grid | the spread over two formulations of the same recursion |
| step | η* identical to 0.0 between order 30 / 1e-32 and order 36 / 1e-38 at 12 points | a step-halving difference (truncation) |
| independent | 2.3e-11 to 5.1e-11 relative in F′ at η = 20 | the difference to an own Dormand–Prince 5(4) in mpmath at 30 digits (tolerance 1e-13) from the same start |

The start error of an inward walk matters through the C-mode: an error dF at η = L in F alone moves (F_∞, C) by (dF, dF·L²), and F(20) by
dF(1 + L²/20⁴); with the series' F and F′ both exact to a relative 1e-16 this is 5e-14 at L = 12800 (measured, raw `dev_09_perturb.log`), not
dF·L⁴/20⁴ = 2e-5, which holds only if F′ moves with F.

## 3. The limit F_∞ → 0 in closed form

The terms of N of lowest total degree in (F, F′, F″) have degree 4 (`n4_factor`, sympy, expanded):

    N₄ = −(ηF″ + F′)² [(η² + 1)(η² + 2) F′² + 2F((η² + 1)F″ + ηF′)].

The first factor is the double root F″ = −F′/η, F = A + B ln η (a log end, p = B). In the second put ψ = 2F′/F; then
(η² + 1)ψ′ + ηψ + (η² + 1)(1 + η²/4)ψ² = 0 (checked symbolically), a Bernoulli equation, and χ = 1/ψ satisfies χ′ − ηχ/(η² + 1) = 1 + η²/4, so

    F′/F = 1/(2χ),  χ = √(η² + 1) h,  h = K + (7/8) asinh η + η√(η² + 1)/8.

At large η this is F′/F = 4/η³ + …, the family of #159, with F/F_∞ = 1 − 2ν² + (c + 7 ln η)ν⁴ and **c = C/F_∞ = 8K + 7 ln 2 + 19/4** (matched by hand;
the convergence below confirms it). For c < c₀ = 7 ln 2 + 19/4 = 9.60 (K < 0) the root η* of h is a singular point, F ~ (η − η*)^α with α = 1/(2χ′(η*)) = 2/(η*² + 4),
#167's exponent; for c → −∞, η*² → −c. For K > 0 there is no root for η > 0, F′/F = 1/(2K) at η = 0 (a regular crossing of the limit equation), and the solution is singular at η = −η_K < 0
(h(−η_K) = 0), so it is not global.

The crate against this, at F_∞ = 0.04, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005 and c = −1e4 … 6 (`closed_form`): the difference of η* is 5.1e-5, 1.3e-5, 3.2e-6, 8.0e-7 at c = 0 (η*₀ = 1.288574873), and falls by
4.00 at every halving for all seven c; at F_∞ = 0.005 the relative difference is at most 2.2e-5 (c = 6: η*₀ = 0.4573391187). This is the planted truth for the small-F_∞ part of the scan: a closed form that
shares with the crate only N₄.

What the limit does not give: for c above c₀ the limit solution is regular at η = 0, but the full flow ends `F->0` for c up to a larger value c* and reaches the floor beyond it: the bisection of
the lines ends at C* = 0.4923 (F_∞ = 0.01, c* = 49.2), 6.151 (F_∞ = 0.1, c* = 61.5) and 4262 (F_∞ = 1), with η* = 1e-6 at the `F->0` end, and for larger c the floor has p ≈ 4F_∞²/C. At the triple coincidence of the cubic's roots
F″ = −F′/η (the double root of N₄) and the finite root, at F′/F = 2/(η(η² + 1)(η² + 2)), η ≈ (4K)^{1/4}, the O(F_∞²) terms split the double root and the walk's root avoids the crossing
(the roots of the cubic along the walk at (0.2, 3·10³), raw `dev_14_roots_0.2_3000.log`). That is the observed mechanism, **not decided**.

and end at `F->0` (63.95, 15.86), and none ends `TINYSTEP` in the run of record (raw `dev_05_dd.log`, `dev_06_dd.log`). The 126 survivors are real log ends to 1e-12 relative;
whether a C¹ solution continues across such an end is the question of #167 §5, not answered here.

## 5. The floor

The walk reaches η = 10⁻⁶ at 188 points. p = ηF′ at 10⁻², 10⁻⁴ and 10⁻⁶ is printed for each (output, FLOOR table):

| F_∞ | floor points | p flat (p(10⁻⁶)/p(10⁻⁴) within 10% of 1) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.001 … 0.07 (8 values) | 11 … 13 each (97) | all |
| 0.1, 0.15, 0.2 | 13, 12, 12 | 12, 11, 10 |
| 0.25, 0.5, −0.5 | 9 each | all |
| 0.75 | 9 | 8 |
| ±1 | 9 each | none (p(10⁻⁶)/p(10⁻⁴) = 5.4 … 9.9) |

For the 165 flat points F′ ≈ p/η, F ≈ F₀ + p ln η, a log end, and **p₀·C/(4F_∞²) lies between 0.838 and 1.303, tending to 1** as C grows (F_∞ = 0.25: 1.0547 at C = 10⁴, 1.0006 at C = 10⁸; F_∞ = 0.001: 0.9999 at C = 10⁵): p₀ ≈ 4F_∞²/C.
The sign of p is that of F_∞ at all 188 points. For F_∞ = ±1 and the other 5 rising points p is not yet constant at 10⁻⁶ (the flow is closing on a singularity below the floor, or is not in the log regime yet): p₀ = 0.138 at C = 10⁴ falls to 7.4e-7 at C = 10⁸ (F_∞ = 1,
p₀C/(4F_∞²) from 344 to 18).
A regular crossing would have F′ bounded at η = 0, p₀ = 0, and would need p to change sign or touch zero between flat floor points of different C at fixed F_∞: p never does, it only falls toward 0 as C → ∞, where F → F_∞ constant (an excluded solution).
So within the grid and the lines, the floor flows are the escaping roots of the cubic (F″ ≈ −F′/η), not the finite root F″(0) = −F′(0)²/F(0) of a regular crossing (#167 §2).

## 6. Not decided and not covered

- **The κ-curve ends.** 126 points end at F² = η² + 1 with F″ ~ ln|Δη|, F and F′ finite (R ≤ 1e-12). #167 §5 leaves open whether a C¹ (or C²) solution continues across such a log end; this scan does not change that. At R ≤ 1e-8 the same label was given to 44 more points, which
  cross the curve with a complex pair of singularities at distance between 1e-12 and 1e-8: the label depends on R, and the 126 are the ones that survive 1e-12 relative.
- **The floor.** The walk stops at η = 10⁻⁶. A singularity below it cannot be told from a log end: 23 of the 188 floor points have p still rising at 10⁻⁶ (F_∞ = ±1 at all C ≥ 10⁴, and 5 points with F_∞ = 0.1 to 0.75), and 4 `F->0` points have η* below 10⁻⁴ (smallest 1.127e-6). The bisection at C* ends at η* = 9.99999e-7 on the `F->0` side because the walk cannot go below the floor.
- **Start.** The series cannot be started (|N|/Σ|terms| > 1e-20 at L = 819200) beyond |C|/|F_∞| ≈ 10⁹; F_∞ is not scanned beyond 128 or below 0.001 in modulus, nor |C| beyond 1e8.
- **The continuum.** 818 points, 607 line points and 5 bisections do not exclude a narrow set of crossings between them; smoothness of η*(C) along the lines is all that supports interpolation.
- **η = 0 without H₀** (#185) and the η < 0 side are not touched.
- Everything is numerical evidence.
