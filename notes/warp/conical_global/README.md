# conical_global (crate)

The Rust side of [conical_global.md](../conical_global.md), [conical_loglog.md](../conical_loglog.md) and
[conical_offgrid.md](../conical_offgrid.md): the implicit cubic N(F, F', F''; x) = 0 of the conical family, its Taylor walk with
a singularity locator, and the matching. `conical_global.py`, `conical_loglog.py` and `conical_offgrid.py` build it
(`cargo build --release`) and run its three binaries (`conical_global`, `loglog`, `offgrid`).

## Layout

```text
src/
├── poly.rs        PolyN (N as a polynomial in F, F', F'', x); implicit_taylor on multishoot::implicit::Recursion
├── hp.rs          PolyQ, HpCfg, hp_walk on multishoot::implicit::Walker, scalar W<P> of any multidouble precision
├── walk.rs        the walk in double-double and the locator (the f64 fit; `LocStats`, the five-step rule `Hist`)
├── loc.rs         feature `locate_lib`: multisolver's `locate` in both walkers (#207)
├── scan.rs        the verdict of one (F_inf, C); model.rs, connect.rs: the equations and the matching
├── offgrid.rs     the verdicts off #167's grid; planted.rs, json.rs: planted equations, JSON output
├── tables.rs      generated by conical_global.py --gen
├── tables_hp.rs   generated by conical_offgrid.py --gen-hp
└── main.rs, bin/  the three binaries
```

## Dependencies

`multishoot =5.0.0` with its `mutate` feature, `multisolver =0.3.0` and `multidouble =6.1.0`, all from crates.io, no git pin. The
`mutate` feature is on for the controls `n-drift` (`ImplicitControl::SkipOrder`) and `recip-dd` (`ImplicitControl::RecipDD`).

## Features

| feature | what |
|---|---|
| `locate_lib` | `multisolver::locate` (the singularity locator of #191 and #206) in place of the f64 Domb-Sykes fit of `walk.rs` and `hp.rs`, in the lane's precision, with the library's window and degree; the verdicts carry a `loc` key |
| `locate_mut_early`, `locate_mut_late` | controls of the feature: the window fixed by hand through `locate_at` (first order 2, or the last 10 orders, degree 2) |
| `locate_ungated` | not a control: `locate` at every step, to check the gate of `loc.rs` |

The scripts build the crate with default features. A feature arm is built with `cargo build --release --features=locate_lib` (add
`CARGO_TARGET_DIR` to keep the arms apart), its three binaries copied to `target/release`, and the script run with `--nobuild`.

### Comparison with #190's baseline

#190's baseline is the feature-off crate on multishoot 4.2.0, multisolver 0.2.2 and multidouble 5.1.0 (Raspberry Pi 5, logs in
`negative-mass-raw/I190-rpi5`). The runs are on the Mac (`negative-mass-raw/I216-mac`, README sections S1 and S2, tables
`step1_table.md` and `step2_table.md` from `sys/summarize.py`, comparisons `cmp_*.txt` from `sys/compare_run.py`, which runs #190's
`compare_outputs.py` unchanged).

| script | feature off (step 1) | feature on (step 1) | one path (step 2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `conical_offgrid.py --grid=ctl --no-controls --dump=...` | 16 of 16; 537 eta* equal to the baseline bit for bit; no class or kind differs | 16 of 16; largest relative difference of an eta* 2.45e-15 | 16 of 16; the same as feature on, bit for bit |
| `conical_offgrid.py --grid=ctl --controls-only --controls=n-drift,recip-dd` | both fire on target only | both fire on target only | both fire on target only |
| `conical_loglog.py` | 20 of 20, 17 of 17 controls on target only | 20 of 20, 17 of 17 | 20 of 20, 17 of 17 |
| `conical_global.py` | 17 of 17, 13 of 13 controls on target only | 17 of 17, 13 of 13 | 17 of 17, 13 of 13 |

In all 12 runs the comparator finds no gated difference: every label, count, class, kind and eta* equals the baseline's as printed,
and each control's own log and exit code equals the baseline's. The one path equals the feature-on arm of step 1 in the three logs and
in the `ctl` dump with no difference at all (the error-sized numbers equal as printed, 0 records with another step count).
Error-sized numbers (residuals at the double-double floor) that differ from the baseline as printed are listed, not gated, and each
is held to its own check's bar; every check passes: 5 of 77 in the `ctl` run, 206 of 490 in `conical_loglog.py`, 4 of 95 in
`conical_global.py` with the feature on and so with one path; with the feature off 16 of 490 in `conical_loglog.py` and none
elsewhere.

`cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` in this directory is clean on the one path. At step 1 it was not: 11 errors, 6 of them in
the recursion and the walk that step 2 deletes (`run_12_clippy_step1_off`, `run_21_clippy_step1_on`).

### Comparison (Mac, `negative-mass-raw/I207-mac`, README for the criteria written before the runs of record)

Baselines are #183's `run_04a` and `run_03b` on the full grid (818 points and their mirrors, lines, bisections) and #190's `ctl`,
`conical_global.py` and `conical_loglog.py` runs, all feature off on the Raspberry Pi. Comparator: #190's `compare_outputs.py`, unchanged.

| script | feature off | feature on |
|---|---|---|
| `conical_offgrid.py --no-controls`, stage a, 15 checks, 818 points (`run_09`, `run_07`) | 15 of 15; 0 gated lines of 349; 4034 eta* equal to 1.3e-15 | **10 of 15**: `regress167`, `scan`, `spread`, `modes`, `bisect` fail; 59 dump records of another class or kind (31 `a3->0` to `F->0`, 25 `a3->0` to `TINYSTEP`, 2 in #167's binary `a3->0` to `NONFINITE`, 1 `fold` to `F->0`), 3971 other eta* within 1.9e-11 |
| stage b, `dd_qd`, 142 points (`run_10`, `run_08`) | 1 of 1; eta* equal | **0 of 1**: (1.5, 1e4) `a3->0` in double-double against `TINYSTEP` in quad-double; the other 141 points equal in class, kind and eta* (worst 4.1e-13 between the lanes) |
| `ctl` grid, 16 checks (`run_15`, `run_06`) | 16 of 16; 0 gated lines; 537 eta* within 1.3e-15 | 15 of 16 (`regress167`: (+-2, +-3000)), 4 records of another class or kind |
| `conical_global.py` (#167) (`run_11`, `run_12`) | 17 of 17, 13 of 13 controls on target; 0 gated lines | 16 of 17 (`scan`: 2 `NONFINITE`), 13 of 13 controls on target |
| `conical_loglog.py` (#184) (`run_13`, `run_14`) | 20 of 20, 17 of 17 controls on target; 0 gated lines | 19 of 20 (`basin`: 9 of 100 labels `kappa@` for `NONFINITE`), 17 of 17 controls on target |

What the feature changes:

- **The exponent.** The fitted alpha(F) of the 509 `F->0` points is within 1.7e-8 (relative) of 2/(eta*^2 + 4); the f64 fit was within
  1.5e-2. At #183's 20 O8 points (alpha 1e-8 to 6e-8) both lanes print the ratio 1.000 to the prediction (the f64 fits: 0.994 to 1.009
  in double-double, 0.997 to 1.000 in quad-double, the latter after a window of 33 of 60 orders chosen for it).
- **Where eta* moved.** 3971 eta* of the dump that keep class and kind are within 1.9e-11 max(1, eta*) of the baseline's (none above
  1e-10; 89 above 1e-12; the largest, at F_inf = 128, are `a3->0` ends). In the planted blow-ups of `plant_167` (truth 0.7321) the f64
  fit is 7.5e-12 off (0.7321000000074667), the library 1e-16 (0.7321000000000001).
- **The kappa curve.** Of #183's 126 `a3->0` records, 116 keep their label. At the other 10 the library reads a conjugate pair
  (Im s from 1.4e-11, alpha(F) = 5/2 in all three fields) where the f64 fit read a real singularity: 5 cross the curve and end `F->0`
  farther in, 5 end `TINYSTEP` (the walk stalls at the old eta*; a pair is read at 38 to 55 of its 73 to 79 gated steps). In #167's
  `walk.rs`, which expands in x without a scaled variable, the walk has no real estimate to stop on and ends `NONFINITE` at R of about
  1.5e-10 with a planned step of 0 (traced at (2, 3000); not examined further). This is what fails the five checks above. The
  cause of the difference between the two readings is not decided.
- **Cost.** Wall time of the runs with the feature on over off: stage a 14 times, stage b 9 times, `conical_global.py` 3.3 times,
  `conical_loglog.py` 3.0 times (the gate keeps `locate` to the steps within 16 stop radii: on the `ctl` grid 67,088 calls against 266,880 and 570 s against 2087 s without it).

Controls of the feature (`ctl` grid, 16 checks): `locate_mut_early` fails `regress167` and `scan` (one `TINYSTEP`, (16, 1e8)) and
differs from the baseline in 11 lines of kind (ii) (2 for the feature); `locate_mut_late` passes 16 of 16 and differs in none. The ungated arm
(`locate_ungated`) equals the gated arm in all 685 records of the `ctl` dump outside the `loc` key, every eta* bit for bit, and in every
line of the log.
